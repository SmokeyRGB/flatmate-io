How to Fill the Themes Board

One column is one User Activity from the story map. One sticky inside it is one Epic. That is the whole rule — the board is just the story map's top two rows, stood upright. Every epic gets a name that works alone on a wall, plus one line underneath so nobody has to open the map to know what it means.

MOVE 1

Copy the backbone across

The five activities become the five columns, left to right, in journey order. Do not rename them here — if a name is wrong, fix it in the story map first.

MOVE 2

Drop the epics in as stickies

Each activity has up to three epics. Name, then one line of plain English. Fifteen stickies total, three per column.

MOVE 3

Leave the 100 stories off

Stories stay in the story map. This board exists to be readable across a room, and 100 stickies is not readable across a room.

Why these names, and not the first ones

See what is expected of me right now → What is next, and who is waiting on me
Get in without friction → Link to first vote in one minute
Land on a time we can all live with → “Tue 17:00 — 5 of 7 can make it”
Welcome them without them reading about themselves → People come and go, the round does not leak
  1. Does it name a thing, not a feeling? “See what is expected of me right now” describes a mood. “What is next, and who is waiting on me” describes the screen you would actually build.
  2. Could it be a screen name? Then it is too technical. “Ranking page” fails; “A ranking I can check, an invite I can send” passes, because it names the outcome rather than the page.
  3. Could it be a single story? Then it is too small. The gloss line carries the detail so the name doesn't have to.

Where the product already has a good phrase, use it verbatim. “Tue 17:00 — 5 of 7 can make it” is lifted straight from 02-SRD.md S-20, and it explains the whole epic faster than any paraphrase.

Why columns are activities, not epics

Putting all fifteen epics across the top gives you fifteen columns nobody can hold in their head. Five is a backbone. The hue runs indigo to rose left to right so the journey direction is visible even from the back of the room.

Do this board before boards 1, 3 and 4

Impact/effort and Now-Next-Later both sort these fifteen stickies. Sorting things you have not named yet is guesswork, and it quietly turns the exercise into estimation.

Nothing here is prioritised yet

Column order is journey order, not importance. Sticky order inside a column is reading order, not sequence. The MVP line has not been drawn — that is the next exercise, not this one.

The sticky test

Goes on this board
  • An outcome a resident would recognise. “A ranking I can check, an invite I can send.”
  • Something that takes more than one story to deliver, but reads as one job.
  • Wording a resident would use, not a name from the schema.
Stays off
  • A single story. “See a heatmap of who can make each slot” is one card inside epic 4.2.
  • A screen or a component. “Ranking page” names a build, not an outcome.
  • Anything only the household admin account can do — wrong persona for this map.
  • System internals: the solver, push delivery, the retention job.
