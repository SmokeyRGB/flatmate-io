## 1. Feature Themes / Epics

The board groups the backlog into **5 epics**. Each epic has 2-3 "goals" (a short outcome statement, in italics, describing what should be true once the goal is met), and under each goal a handful of small user-story stickies ("I want to... / I can...").

### EP-A — (Late) Joining a Casting Round

- **Invite to first vote in one minute**
  _Goal: a new resident who gets the link registers within a minute, and gets straight to participation, on whatever device they own._
  - open the link and see which WG I am joining before I confirm
  - join with just a name and a password
  - get to my first vote without an email address
  - not be asked to install anything before my first vote
  - add an email later so I can get back in if I lose my password
  - turn on push notifications after my first vote, not before
  - get push notifications on important topics

- **What is next, and who is waiting on me**
  _Goal: after three days away, I know the one thing to do next and why, without thinking._
  - see one screen that tells me what to do next
  - see exactly one main action, not a list I have to sort myself
  - read why each task is first — "4 others are waiting on your note"
  - see how much time is left when the round has a deadline
  - see "5 of 7 have voted" so I know whether the group is waiting on me

- **What happened while I was away**
  _Goal: I can reconstruct what the WG decided while I was offline, in one screen._
  - read one feed that answers "what happened while I was away"
  - see outcomes in that feed, not every small action someone took
  - jump from a feed entry straight to the thing it is about
  - never see a feed entry for something I am not allowed to open

### EP-B — Every Application in One Place

- **Open a Casting-round: which rooms, by when, who votes**
  _Goal: a round that matches reality and survives rooms being filled one at a time._
  - open a round for the rooms we are actually casting for
  - set a soft deadline so people know when to act
  - see who is taking part in this round
  - keep the round running for the other rooms when one is taken
  - reopen a closed round when an accepted applicant pulls out
  - have that deadline nudge without ever blocking a decision

- **Paste it or type it, same card either way**
  _Goal: every application becomes the same record regardless of channel, with a human always confirming._
  - type an application into a form when it arrived by phone or in person
  - paste a message and have name, age, contact and text pulled out
  - confirm or correct every field the parser guessed before it is saved
  - get the form with my pasted text still in it, not an error, when the parser finds nothing
  - link a person's earlier application to their new one
  - save an application with nothing but a name

- **Know where the data came from**
  _Goal: for any applicant we can say where the data came from, and, if they request it, hand it over._
  - record whether the details came from the applicant or from someone else
  - be told about the one-month notice duty when the data came from a third party
  - delete a single application without touching the rest of the round
  - produce a data export for one applicant when they ask what we hold

### EP-C — Pick Who to Invite

- **Card by card, four ratings, two minutes**
  _Goal: every resident gets through all open applications in one sitting and knows what their vote meant._
  - go through the open applications card by card in one sitting
  - pick one of four ratings (_No, Rather not, Like, Must have_)
  - change my vote while the round is still open
  - see "nothing is waiting for you" when I am done
  - install the app on my phone when I choose to, not before

- **Too many "must haves" get a second pass**
  - be shown a short second pass only when I have given more "must have" ratings than the round's favourite budget allows
  - rate my own shortlist with the same cards I already know
  - see how long I have left when the round has a soft deadline

- **A ranking I can check, an invite I can send**
  _Goal: the group's votes become a ranking nobody can dispute, and based on it, it is easy to send an invitation._
  - see a candidate's score only after I have voted on that candidate
  - see how the score is worked out, not just the number
  - see candidates with too few votes in a separate "waiting for votes" section (to avoid high scores based on a single vote)
  - mark a candidate as invited
  - see when a vote came from someone who has since moved out
  - see every candidate's current stage in one place

### EP-D — Organize In-Person Casting

- **"Tuesdays from 16:00" becomes a real time window**
  _Goal: everyone's real availability is in the system as structured windows._
  - enter when I can and cannot in a grid on my phone
  - write "Tuesdays from 16:00" and confirm what the app understood
  - never have a guessed time window saved without me confirming it
  - enter an applicant's preferred times by hand when no link was used
  - see all slots treated as available (default, if nothing was entered)
  - provide applicants with a link to enter their availability for a casting
  - revoke my availability, e.g. when sick or having another appointment unexpectedly

- **"Tue 17:00 — 5 of 7 can make it"**
  _Goal: the group lands on a time that works and can see why it works, including when the automatic solver is unavailable._
  - see a heatmap of how many of us can make each slot
  - see slots greyed out that this applicant cannot make anyway
  - place slots by hand when I would rather not compute anything
  - set our house rules once (e.g. "not before 18:00", "at most N castings a day")
  - press "calculate a proposal" and read why those slots were picked

- **Lock the date, then inform the ones who were not there how it went**
  _Goal: the people who could not attend can participate as if they had been there._
  - confirm one slot as the casting appointment for everyone
  - confirm it even when not everyone has reacted
  - be reminded after the casting to write a few sentences for the others
  - read the notes even though I was not there
  - see "no notes yet" instead of an empty field when nobody has written (prompt flatmates for notes, e.g. "Tom has asked for information on how it went")

### EP-E — Decide, Offer, Welcome the New Flatmate

- **Round two, with a veto that ranks instead of deletes**
  _Goal: the group decides on an offer, and one person can say a strong no without blocking everyone._
  - vote on the people we actually met, with the same four ratings
  - see the round-two ranking in the same layout as the first, but over only the people we actually met
  - place a veto with a reason when I strongly disagree
  - see that my veto ranks a candidate low but does not delete them
  - not be able to veto once an offer has already gone out
  - be told honestly that an anonymous veto in a WG of five is not anonymous

- **Make the offer, and survive a no after a yes**
  _Goal: an offer goes out, the answer comes back into the system, and the process survives every way it can go wrong._
  - make an offer and record the room and the planned move-in date
  - record the applicant answer, whether it is yes or no
  - see the status branch back to offer-pending or declined when someone pulls out after accepting, audited either way
  - record a move-in date that is still provisional
  - get a ready copy-paste text for the offer
  - see all casting appointments and move-in dates in one calendar

- **People come and go, the round does not leak**
  _Goal: the round survives its own membership changing, without anyone reading what they should not._
  - never read a vote, veto or note that was written about me
  - send one invite link that lets an accepted applicant become a flatmate on their own (with ID-linking to their application for retention)
  - lose access to the round the day I move out
  - know my votes still count after I have gone
  - close the round once every room is taken

## 2. Roadmap

Section 3 of the board lays the same 5 epics out on a release timeline with four lanes:

| Lane | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **v0.1 – NOW** | Being built first |
| **v0.2 – NEXT** | Right after |
| **v1.x – LATER** | Further out |
| **v2.x – SOMEDAY** | Idea only, not scheduled |

**Correction from an earlier version of this file:** the first pass at this extraction wrongly concluded "everything is in v0.1". A later pass corrected that to "each epic's core feature (its 'goal') ships in v0.1, only the stories underneath it move right" — but per `02-SRD.md` §5.4, which governs this cut, that is wrong too: **not every epic's core feature ships in v0.1**. EP-D's three features (scheduling) and EP-E's three features (decide/offer) are all v0.2. Only EP-A, EP-B and EP-C have their core feature in v0.1, and even for those, many of the individual user stories underneath that goal are placed further right on the board, into v0.2 or v1.x. So the sortation happens at the story level within an epic, not uniformly at the epic level — and where this file and §5.4 disagree, §5.4 governs. Nothing on the board is currently placed in v2.x – Someday.

Quick overview — how many rows per epic land in each lane. **The goal/feature row is counted in
whichever lane it actually sits in**, which is no longer always v0.1: EP-D's three goals and
EP-E's three goals are all v0.2, because the scheduling half and the decision half are outside
the v0.1 vertical slice (`02-SRD.md` §5.4). EP-D therefore has nothing in v0.1 at all, and EP-E
has only the one row that is really S-31, the visibility invariant.

| Epic | v0.1 – Now | v0.2 – Next | v1.x – Later |
|---|---|---|---|
| EP-A. (Late) Joining a Casting Round | 10 | 9 | 0 |
| EP-B. Every Application in One Place | 10 | 6 | 3 |
| EP-C. Pick Who to Invite | 8 | 8 | 0 |
| EP-D. Organize In-Person Casting | 0 | 19 | 1 |
| EP-E. Decide, Offer, Welcome the New Flatmate | 1 | 15 | 4 |
